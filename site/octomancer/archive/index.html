The Story So Far

In the 2020 lockdown, stuck at home in Brno, we (Daniel and Milan Švancara) started a two-person studio, Brotagonists, and built a multiplayer octopus game together.

  • Dec 2020Closed beta goes out to friends.
  • Feb 2021The Octomancer website goes live.
  • Jun 2021Open beta: anyone can play.

Over the next couple of years, Octomancer grew into a single-player adventure: Octopus Adventure. It opens with a short tutorial, sends you back to a hub with a shop between runs, and drops you into an endless procedural dungeon — with a Beholder always somewhere behind you.

That's the game you can download and play today.
