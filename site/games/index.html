Thinking with Portals VR

Play with portals in VR

The first three test chambers of Portal rebuilt in VR, plus a playground to mess around in, with controls modelled on Half-Life: Alyx.

Under the hood is a custom render pipeline for recursive portal rendering in VR, so you can look through one portal into another. Windows demo on itch.io.
