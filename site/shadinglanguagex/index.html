Share to the gallery

Images

    
Sample them in the shader with image("name.png", texcoord = texcoord()). A path like textures\albedo.tif finds albedo.png too. Shared projects take their images along.

    

    
    
    
    
    You can also drop images onto the code editor. Big images are scaled down to fit. To replace an image, remove it first.
