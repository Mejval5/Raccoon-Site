I walked the Camino Portuguese!

August 2021

I went on a big journey this summer. First, I rode my motorcycle to Portugal. I bought the famous BMW F 650 GS, and a week later departed for the biggest adventure of my life. The ride was exhausting, but a two-week stop at my mom’s house in Switzerland to regain my strength let me continue.

In Portugal, I put on my trusty little backpack and started walking. My legs ached, but soon I picked up the pace and felt more energetic with each day. It didn’t take long, and I was finally in Santiago.

I picked up my motorcycle and continued my journey back to the Czech Republic.
