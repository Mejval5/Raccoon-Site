## 1. The game in one paragraph

You are the octopus, dropped into an underwater dungeon that goes down forever. You swim with real momentum (the original's swim maths), dash through gaps and through piranhas, bomb soft rock open to reach pearls and rare shells, and keep descending, because after two minutes the Beholder comes down the shaft behind you and it never stops. Score is depth plus pickups plus kills; you die, you see your score against your best, you tap **Swim again** and get a fresh cave. **Simpler** means: no tutorial, no hub, no shop, no timeline intro, no dark levels, no liquids, no grab/throw, no puzzles (buttons, cables, gates, pushable blocks), no ice walls, no electro rocks, no coins or gold, and no layered parallax in play (one background, one vignette, one slow noise, done). **More fun** means: two buttons instead of four (Dash is also the attack, Bomb is the tool), bombs that break rock and are dangerous to you too, an infinite seeded world instead of 10x16 rooms, Milan's 2021 creatures back as enemies, Milan's music, an always-visible score and best, restart in one tap, and juice. It looks like Octomancer: Milan's cave, Milan's creatures, and Milan's octopus with its real swim animation.

## 2. Tech decisions (Beaver owns; deviations need his line in `web/NIGHT-LOG.md`)

- **Stack.** Static files under `play/`, no build step, no npm at runtime, no WebAssembly, no library. ES modules with a flat import graph and `<link rel="modulepreload">` for every module. Canvas 2D, one canvas, HUD and menus as HTML/CSS. JSDoc, no TypeScript. Offline tools (Python + Pillow, ffmpeg) live in `web/tools/` and never ship.
- **Loop.** rAF with a fixed 50 Hz accumulator (dt 0.02, the original's step), max 5 steps per frame, interpolated render, pause on `visibilitychange`/blur. No per-frame allocation in the hot loop: pools for particles, projectiles and enemies.
- **Physics.** Own, ~250 lines: semi-implicit Euler, Box2D drag `v *= 1/(1+dt*drag)`, impulses `v += J/m`, circle vs tile grid (push out along the normal, kill normal velocity, keep tangential), sub-step when `|v|*dt > r/2`, circle/AABB overlap for everything else.
- **Octopus body and swim, from the Unity scene, not the bomb prefab:** mass 2, linear drag 2, gravity scale 0.01, rotation frozen (`unity/../Scenes/MainGame.unity:39451-39466`), collider fitted as a circle r ≈ 0.45 (from the 20-point polygon at `:39472`, report the fit). Swim (`unity/Scripts/Player/SquidMovementScript.cs:98-117`, `ScriptableObjects/Player/Variables/Octopus Movement/SquidSettings.asset`): push 80 per second as an impulse per step, max speed 6, acceleration cap `1-(v/max)^10`, turn 450°/s, turn delay 0.5 s, rest rotate 10, joystick curve `j*|j|^0.5/2`. Dash impulse 20 (`MainGame.unity:39526`). Tonight's deviation: keyboard input is normalised to |v| ≤ 1 (the original's raw `GetAxis` diagonals push 1.7x harder; fairness between thumb and keys wins). Camera: at least 18x24 world units visible, as `unity/Scripts/Camera/CameraPos.cs:108-147`; 1 tile = 1 unit.
- **Octopus look.** M1 draws a **code placeholder** (head + 8 bezier tentacles). Track B replaces it with **Milan's real animation baked offline** from the Creature pack (`DECISIONS` §4): one sprite sheet (body only, ≤ 48 frames: Swim05, Idle4, Idle4Swirl for hurt; dash = Swim05 played fast with a squash) plus separate eye sprites (open, closed, angry) placed per frame from baked eye anchors, so blink and angry are code-driven. Runtime cost: one rotated `drawImage` for the body and two for the eyes. The placeholder stays behind `?octo=code` as the fallback. No runtime mesh, no Creature code.
- **Input** from the first hour, one module: keyboard (arrows/WASD move, Z/Enter/Shift dash, Space/X bomb, Esc pause) and touch (floating joystick on the left half, Dash and Bomb buttons bottom-right, Pointer Events, `touch-action:none`, safe-area insets). Gameplay only ever sees `{move:{x,y}, dash, bomb, pause}` with pressed/held flags. Mouse: menus only.
- **World.** Seeded (`mulberry32`, `?seed=N`), pure generator, chunk ring buffer. Deterministic: same seed, same cave.
- **Saves.** `localStorage["octomancer.best.v1"]` = `{v:1, best, runs, muted}`, every access in try/catch with an in-memory fallback.
- **Budgets.** In the Chromium pane at 375x812 (no CPU throttle available): frame work ≤ 4 ms median, ≤ 8 ms p95 (a 4x slower phone still makes 16.7 ms). Transfer to playable ≤ 1.5 MB; total ≤ 3 MB **without music**; music ≤ 2 MB more, one format, fetched only after the first input. Nothing loads from a CDN except Google Fonts already used by the site.
- **Test hooks, mandatory.** `window.__octo = {state(), step(n), input(actions), reset(seed), metrics(), spawn(kind,x,y), autoDive(on)}` and `?fps=1` overlay, `?debug=1` hitboxes and chunk borders. `play/tests/index.html` runs unit checks and writes `PASS n/n` or `FAIL ...` into `#result`.
