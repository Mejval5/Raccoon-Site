Bug found and fixed during this session's own verification: the tool's
first pass compared each triangle's *vertex ids* (0-266) against 1044 to
decide "is this a body triangle", but the .json's region start_index/
end_index are positions *within the flat 1200-entry triangle-index array*,
not vertex ids (267 vertices can't have an index of 1043). That comparison
was always true, so every eye triangle (52 of them) was ALSO warped onto
the body sheet using its tiny eye-region atlas UVs, producing a dark
patch over both eye sockets (visible at DPR 2, see the "before" note in
NIGHT-LOG.md). Fixed by filtering on each triangle's position in the flat
index array instead; re-verified with a fresh bake + DPR2 screenshot
(clean, no patch) before wiring it in.
